# Code of Conduct

PARE aims to be a constructive place for contributors and users. Be respectful, assume good faith, give specific technical feedback, and make room for people with different experience levels. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainer Ayush Raj may edit or remove harmful content, close conversations, or restrict participation when needed. Decisions will consider context and impact; no fixed response-time promise is made.

If you experience or witness a concern, email Ayush Raj at [ayush_24a12res897@iitp.ac.in](mailto:ayush_24a12res897@iitp.ac.in) with `PARE conduct report` in the subject. Do not post personal details in a public issue. Reports will be handled with discretion, though absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.
